## Brand marks & placeholders (`*.svg`)

`logo.svg` and the remaining `*.svg` files are **generated/owned** artwork created for
this kit. The product `.svg` placeholders predate the photography and are retained only
as a fallback; the demos render the `.jpg` photos.

## Why photos

The demos exist to look like a **real storefront** — flat vector placeholders read as a
mockup. Token repainting is demonstrated through the chrome (nav, buttons, headings,
prices, badges, borders), which is exactly how a real e-commerce site behaves: product
photography doesn't recolor with the theme.
